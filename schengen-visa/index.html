What is a Schengen Visa?

A Schengen visa (Type C) is a short-stay visa that allows Indian passport holders to travel freely within the 27 Schengen member countries in Europe for up to 90 days within any 180-day period. The visa is applied for at the embassy or consulate of the main destination country, or the first point of entry if visiting multiple Schengen countries equally.

The Schengen Area includes countries such as France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Greece, Switzerland, Austria, Belgium, Portugal, Sweden, and others. Schengen visas are physical sticker visas placed in the passport.

Visa decisions are made solely by the respective Schengen member state embassy. Approval cannot be guaranteed.

Who Can Apply?

Indian passport holders who wish to visit Europe for tourism, visiting family or friends, attending cultural events, or short business trips may apply for a Schengen Type C visa. Applications must be submitted 15 days to 6 months before the intended travel date.

Documents Required

Key documents for a Schengen visa from India typically include:

  • Valid passport with at least 3 months validity beyond the return date and at least 2 blank pages
  • Completed Schengen visa application form
  • Recent biometric passport photographs (35mm x 45mm white background)
  • Schengen travel health insurance covering the entire trip (minimum EUR 30,000 coverage)
  • Bank statements for the last 3 to 6 months showing sufficient funds
  • Income tax returns for 3 years
  • Confirmed return flight tickets and hotel bookings
  • Employment letter with salary, NOC, and leave approval
  • Evidence of ties to India (family, employment, property)

Requirements vary between individual Schengen member state embassies. Always verify with the relevant embassy before applying.

Application Process

  1. Identify the main destination country or first point of entry.
  2. Book a biometrics appointment at the relevant Schengen embassy or VFS Global centre.
  3. Complete the Schengen visa application form.
  4. Attend the appointment and submit all documents along with the application fee.
  5. Wait for the embassy to process and return the passport with the visa sticker.

Processing Time

Skypass Visa Services completes documentation preparation within a maximum of 5 working days. Schengen embassies typically process applications within 15 to 25 working days from the biometric appointment. Applications should be submitted at least 3 weeks before the intended travel date.

Common Reasons for Refusal

Schengen visa applications are frequently refused when the embassy is not satisfied of the applicant's genuine visitor intention or financial capacity. Common reasons include:

  • Insufficient proof of financial means
  • Weak ties to India — inadequate evidence of employment, family, or property
  • Missing or inadequate travel health insurance
  • Vague travel purpose or missing itinerary
  • Previous visa refusals or Schengen overstays
