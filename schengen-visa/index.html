What is a Schengen Visa?

A Schengen visa (Type C) is a short-stay visa that allows Indian passport holders to travel freely within the 27 Schengen member countries in Europe for up to 90 days within any 180-day period. The visa is applied for at the embassy or consulate of the main destination country, or the first point of entry if visiting multiple Schengen countries equally.

The Schengen Area includes countries such as France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Greece, Switzerland, Austria, Belgium, Portugal, Sweden, and others. Schengen visas are physical sticker visas placed in the passport.

Visa decisions are made solely by the respective Schengen member state embassy. Approval cannot be guaranteed.

Who Can Apply?

Indian passport holders who wish to visit Europe for tourism, visiting family or friends, attending cultural events, or short business trips may apply for a Schengen Type C visa. Applications must be submitted 15 days to 6 months before the intended travel date.

Documents Required

Key documents for a Schengen visa from India typically include:

  • Valid passport with at least 3 months validity beyond the return date and at least 2 blank pages
  • Completed Schengen visa application form
  • Recent biometric passport photographs (35mm x 45mm white background)
  • Schengen travel health insurance covering the entire trip (minimum EUR 30,000 coverage)
  • Bank statements for the last 3 to 6 months showing sufficient funds
  • Income tax returns for 3 years
  • Confirmed return flight tickets and hotel bookings
  • Employment letter with salary, NOC, and leave approval
  • Evidence of ties to India (family, employment, property)

Requirements vary between individual Schengen member state embassies. Always verify with the relevant embassy before applying.

Application Process

  1. Identify the main destination country or first point of entry.
  2. Book a biometrics appointment at the relevant Schengen embassy or VFS Global centre.
  3. Complete the Schengen visa application form.
  4. Attend the appointment and submit all documents along with the application fee.
  5. Wait for the embassy to process and return the passport with the visa sticker.

Processing Time

Skypass Visa Services completes documentation preparation within a maximum of 5 working days. Schengen embassies typically process applications within 15 to 25 working days from the biometric appointment. Applications should be submitted at least 3 weeks before the intended travel date.

Common Reasons for Refusal

Schengen visa applications are frequently refused when the embassy is not satisfied of the applicant's genuine visitor intention or financial capacity. Common reasons include:

  • Insufficient proof of financial means
  • Weak ties to India — inadequate evidence of employment, family, or property
  • Missing or inadequate travel health insurance
  • Vague travel purpose or missing itinerary
  • Previous visa refusals or Schengen overstays

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Schengen Area visa services.

What is a Schengen visa and which countries does it cover?
A Schengen visa (Type C short-stay visa) allows Indian passport holders to travel freely across 27 European countries in the Schengen Area for up to 90 days within any 180-day period. The member countries include France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Greece, Portugal, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Norway, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Poland, Hungary, Slovenia, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Croatia. A single Schengen visa covers all 27 countries.
Which Schengen country should I apply through from India?
Apply through the embassy of the country where you will spend the most nights. If you are spending equal time in multiple countries, apply through the country of your first entry. For example, if you are spending 7 days in France, 5 days in Italy, and 3 days in Germany, apply through the French Embassy. Each Schengen country handles its own visa applications — there is no central Schengen visa authority.
What documents are required for a Schengen visa from Kerala?
Key documents include: valid passport (at least 3 months validity beyond your departure from Schengen), Schengen visa application form, recent photographs meeting the specific country's specs, confirmed return flight tickets, day-by-day travel itinerary, accommodation bookings for all nights, Schengen travel insurance (minimum EUR 30,000 medical cover), bank statements for 3–6 months, employment certificate with salary, Income Tax Returns, and evidence of ties to India.
What travel insurance is required for a Schengen visa?
Schengen visa applications require travel insurance with a minimum coverage of EUR 30,000 for medical emergencies and repatriation, valid for the entire Schengen Area and the full duration of your trip. The policy must be from an insurer recognised in the Schengen Area. Many Indian insurers offer Schengen-compliant policies. Skypass can advise on suitable insurance options.
How long does Schengen visa processing take from India?
Schengen visa processing typically takes 15 to 25 working days from the date of appointment submission, though the EU regulation allows embassies up to 15 calendar days. Some embassies take longer during peak seasons. Skypass completes document preparation in up to 5 working days. Apply at least 6–8 weeks before your travel date.
What financial proof do I need for a Schengen visa?
There is no universal official minimum bank balance for a Schengen visa. Each Schengen country sets its own guidance. As a general reference, some embassies suggest EUR 50–100 per day of stay, but what matters most is demonstrating that your funds are consistent with your planned expenses. Bank statements for 3–6 months (savings and salary account), ITR, salary slips, and property documents together build a credible financial picture.
Do I need biometrics for a Schengen visa?
Yes. All Schengen visa applicants (first-time and those whose previous biometrics have expired — typically after 59 months) must provide fingerprints and a photograph at a VFS Global Schengen Visa Application Centre. In Kerala, VFS Global centres for Schengen applications are available in Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram. Children under 12 are exempt from fingerprinting.
What are common reasons for Schengen visa refusal?
Common refusal reasons include: insufficient financial means for the planned trip, weak ties to India, an unclear or unbelievable itinerary, missing or inadequate travel insurance, prior Schengen overstay, prior refusals not disclosed, and inconsistencies between the application and supporting documents.
Can I get a multiple-entry Schengen visa?
Yes. If you have a good travel history and have used previous Schengen visas without overstaying, you may be granted a multiple-entry Schengen visa valid for 1 to 5 years, allowing multiple visits as long as each stay does not exceed 90 days within any 180-day period. First-time applicants are typically granted a single or double-entry visa.
