What is the South Korea C-3 Tourist Visa?

Indian passport holders need a C-3 (Short-term General) visa to visit South Korea for tourism, visiting friends or relatives, or attending cultural events. The C-3-9 is the specific sub-category for tourism, allowing stays of up to 90 days. It is issued as a sticker visa and is typically single-entry, though multiple-entry may be granted in certain cases. South Korea does not currently offer a standard e-visa for Indian nationals via the Embassy in India.

Source: Embassy of the Republic of Korea in India

Who Can Apply for a South Korea Tourist Visa?

Any Indian national with a valid passport may apply for a South Korea C-3 visa. The Embassy evaluates applications individually. Factors that strengthen an application include:

  • Stable employment or business activity with approved leave
  • Sufficient personal savings to cover all trip expenses
  • Strong family ties in India (spouse, dependants, property ownership)
  • Prior international travel history (not mandatory but helpful)
  • A clear, day-by-day travel itinerary with confirmed bookings

Note: Meeting these criteria does not guarantee visa approval. All visa decisions are made by the Embassy of the Republic of Korea.

Documents Required for South Korea Visa from India

The following documents are typically required for a C-3 tourist visa. Verify the latest requirements with the Korean Embassy in India or the VFS Korea portal before applying.

  • Valid Indian passport (minimum 6 months validity beyond travel dates)
  • Completed Korea visa application form
  • Recent passport-sized photographs (white background)
  • Confirmed return flight tickets
  • Hotel bookings or accommodation proof for all nights
  • Bank statements for the last 3 to 6 months
  • Employment certificate or NOC with approved leave letter (salaried applicants)
  • Business registration and income documents (self-employed applicants)
  • Detailed day-by-day itinerary
  • Income tax returns (ITR) for the previous year, if applicable

South Korea Visa Application Process from Kerala

Applications from Kerala are processed through the Embassy of Korea in Chennai or New Delhi, or via VFS Global Korea centres. The general steps are:

  1. Consult Skypass — profile review and document gap analysis
  2. Document preparation — Skypass assists with checklisting and reviewing your file (up to 5 working days)
  3. Book VFS appointment — appointment required at VFS Korea centres (available in Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Kolkata, New Delhi, Bengaluru)
  4. Submit application — attend in person with all documents
  5. Embassy processing — typically 5 to 10 working days after submission
  6. Passport collection — collect from VFS or opt for courier return

South Korea Visa Processing Time

The Embassy of Korea typically processes C-3 visa applications within 5 to 10 working days after submission through VFS Global. Skypass completes document preparation in up to 5 working days. Total time from starting your file is approximately 10 to 20 working days, subject to Embassy workload and individual case complexity.

These are estimates only. Embassy decisions may take longer. Apply well in advance of your intended travel date.

Common Reasons for South Korea Visa Refusal

South Korea C-3 visa refusals often occur due to one or more of the following:

  • Insufficient bank balance relative to trip cost and duration
  • Vague or incomplete travel itinerary
  • Missing employer NOC or leave approval letter
  • No clear reason to return to India (weak ties to home country)
  • Prior visa refusals not disclosed in the application
  • Inconsistencies between submitted documents

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about South Korea visa services.

What type of visa do I need to visit South Korea from India?
Indian nationals need a C-3 (Short-term General) visa for tourism in South Korea. The specific sub-category for tourism is C-3-9, allowing stays of up to 90 days. It is issued as a sticker visa, typically single-entry.
What documents are required for South Korea tourist visa from Kerala?
Key documents include: valid passport (6+ months validity), completed Korea visa application form, passport photographs, confirmed return flight tickets, hotel bookings, bank statements for 3–6 months, employer NOC with leave approval, and a detailed day-by-day itinerary. Always verify requirements with the Embassy of Korea.
How long does South Korea visa processing take from India?
The Embassy of Korea typically processes C-3 visa applications within 5 to 10 working days after submission through VFS Global. Skypass completes document preparation in up to 5 working days. Apply at least 3–4 weeks before your travel date.
Is there an e-visa or visa on arrival for South Korea from India?
South Korea does not currently offer a standard tourist e-visa for Indian nationals through the Embassy in India, nor is there a visa on arrival. Applications must be submitted in advance through VFS Global Korea or the Korean Embassy/Consulate.
What is the South Korea C-3 visa fee for Indian applicants?
The Korean Embassy visa fee for a single-entry C-3 visa is approximately ₹3,000–₹5,000, plus VFS Global service charges. Fees are set by the Embassy and VFS and are subject to change. Contact Skypass for the current fee schedule.
Can I extend my South Korea C-3 visa once inside Korea?
In limited circumstances, short-term visas may be extended from within South Korea through the local immigration office (Hi Korea portal). However, extensions are not routinely granted for tourism purposes. Plan your travel within your approved visa duration.
Why do South Korea visa applications get refused?
Common refusal reasons include: insufficient bank balance for the trip duration, vague itinerary, missing employer NOC, weak ties to India, undisclosed prior refusals, and inconsistencies between submitted documents.
