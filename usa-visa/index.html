What is the USA B1/B2 Visitor Visa?

The USA B1/B2 visa is a non-immigrant visitor visa issued by the United States Embassy. The B1 category covers temporary business visits such as conferences, negotiations, and consultations. The B2 category covers tourism, holidays, visiting family or friends, and medical treatment.

For Indian passport holders, the B1/B2 visa is typically issued as a multiple-entry sticker visa valid for up to 10 years. Each permitted stay in the USA is usually up to 6 months as determined by the US Customs and Border Protection officer at the port of entry.

Visa decisions are made solely by the United States Embassy or Consulate. Approval cannot be guaranteed.

Who Can Apply?

Indian passport holders who wish to visit the USA for tourism, visiting family or friends, attending events, or short business trips may apply for a B1/B2 visa. Applicants must demonstrate strong ties to India, sufficient funds to cover the trip, and a clear intention to return home after the visit.

Documents Required

To apply for a USA B-1/B-2 Visitor Visa, key required documents include:

  • Valid passport & previous passports (with travel history where applicable)
  • Recent US-spec photographs & DS-160 application confirmation
  • Recent bank statements, salary slips / income evidence, and ITR documents
  • Employment certificate / NOC & leave approval (or business registration documents)
  • Proposed travel itinerary, accommodation details, or invitation letter (if visiting family)
  • Evidence of strong home-country ties (family commitments, employment, property/assets)

Document requirements may change. Always verify with the US Embassy in India before applying.

Application Process

  1. Complete the DS-160 online application form on the CEAC portal.
  2. Pay the MRV (Machine Readable Visa) fee.
  3. Schedule a visa interview appointment at the nearest US Embassy or Consulate.
  4. Attend the visa interview with all required documents.
  5. If approved, the passport with visa sticker is returned by post or for collection.

Processing Time

Skypass Visa Services completes documentation preparation within a maximum of 5 working days. The US Embassy typically takes 15 to 25 working days after the interview to process the application and return the passport. Processing times may vary based on embassy workload and individual circumstances.

Common Reasons for Refusal

US B2 visas are frequently refused under Section 214(b) when the consular officer is not satisfied that the applicant has strong ties to India. Common reasons include:

  • Insufficient proof of financial stability or employment
  • Weak evidence of family, property, or other ties to India
  • Vague or inconsistent travel purpose
  • Previous visa refusals or immigration violations
  • Incomplete or inconsistent documentation

Skypass thoroughly reviews each applicant's profile before submission to identify and address potential concerns.

Visa Fee

The US Embassy charges a non-refundable MRV application fee (currently USD 185 for B1/B2 visa as of 2024, subject to change). This is a government fee paid directly to the US Embassy and is separate from Skypass service charges. Please verify the current fee on the US Embassy in India website before applying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about USA visa services.

What is the USA B1/B2 visa and who needs it?
The USA B1/B2 visa is a non-immigrant visa. B1 is for business visits (meetings, conferences); B2 is for tourism, visiting family, or medical treatment. Indian passport holders require this visa to enter the United States. It is typically issued as a combined B1/B2 visa valid for up to 10 years with multiple entries, though each stay is usually limited to 6 months.
What documents are required for a USA B1/B2 visa from India?
Key documents include: valid passport (6+ months validity), DS-160 application form, MRV fee payment receipt, appointment confirmation, recent passport photographs, bank statements for 3–6 months, employment certificate with salary details, Income Tax Returns, property documents, and evidence of family ties in India. Requirements vary by profile — salaried applicants, business owners, students, and retired persons need different supporting documents.
What is the DS-160 form for a USA visa?
The DS-160 is the Online Nonimmigrant Visa Application form required for all US non-immigrant visa applicants. It is completed on the US Department of State website (ceac.state.gov) and must be submitted before you can book an interview appointment at the US Embassy or Consulate. The DS-160 barcode number is required when booking the interview.
Is a USA visa interview mandatory for Indians?
Yes, almost all Indian applicants for a B1/B2 visa must attend an in-person interview at the US Embassy (New Delhi) or one of the Consulates (Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata). Interview waiver (dropbox) is available for certain renewal applicants who meet specific eligibility criteria. The consular officer's decision is final.
How long does USA B1/B2 visa processing take from India?
Processing time depends on the interview wait time at your chosen consulate, which fluctuates significantly — it can range from a few weeks to several months. After the interview, visa issuance typically takes 2 to 5 working days. Check current wait times on the US Embassy India website and apply as early as possible.
What financial documents do I need for a USA visa?
There is no officially mandated minimum bank balance for a US B1/B2 visa. What matters is demonstrating that you have sufficient funds to cover your trip and strong reasons to return to India. Typical financial documents include: bank statements for 3–6 months (savings and current accounts), salary slips, Form 16, Income Tax Returns for 2–3 years, and any investment or property documents. Skypass will advise on what is appropriate for your specific profile.
What are the most common reasons for USA visa refusal?
Most US B1/B2 visa refusals happen under Section 214(b) — the applicant failed to demonstrate sufficient ties to India or intent to return. Other reasons include: inconsistent answers during the interview, weak financial profile, prior US visa refusals, overstay history, and unclear purpose of visit. A strong application addresses these concerns with solid documentation.
Can my parents visit the USA on a B2 visa?
Yes, retired parents can apply for a US B2 visitor visa. The key challenge for retired applicants is demonstrating strong ties to India and sufficient financial means (either their own or through financial sponsorship by a child in the US). A strong sponsorship letter and evidence of property, pension, or other ties to India are important. Skypass can help retired applicants prepare a compelling file.
