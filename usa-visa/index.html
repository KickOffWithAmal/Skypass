What is the USA B1/B2 Visitor Visa?

The USA B1/B2 visa is a non-immigrant visitor visa issued by the United States Embassy. The B1 category covers temporary business visits such as conferences, negotiations, and consultations. The B2 category covers tourism, holidays, visiting family or friends, and medical treatment.

For Indian passport holders, the B1/B2 visa is typically issued as a multiple-entry sticker visa valid for up to 10 years. Each permitted stay in the USA is usually up to 6 months as determined by the US Customs and Border Protection officer at the port of entry.

Visa decisions are made solely by the United States Embassy or Consulate. Approval cannot be guaranteed.

Who Can Apply?

Indian passport holders who wish to visit the USA for tourism, visiting family or friends, attending events, or short business trips may apply for a B1/B2 visa. Applicants must demonstrate strong ties to India, sufficient funds to cover the trip, and a clear intention to return home after the visit.

Documents Required

To apply for a USA B-1/B-2 Visitor Visa, key required documents include:

  • Valid passport & previous passports (with travel history where applicable)
  • Recent US-spec photographs & DS-160 application confirmation
  • Recent bank statements, salary slips / income evidence, and ITR documents
  • Employment certificate / NOC & leave approval (or business registration documents)
  • Proposed travel itinerary, accommodation details, or invitation letter (if visiting family)
  • Evidence of strong home-country ties (family commitments, employment, property/assets)

Document requirements may change. Always verify with the US Embassy in India before applying.

Application Process

  1. Complete the DS-160 online application form on the CEAC portal.
  2. Pay the MRV (Machine Readable Visa) fee.
  3. Schedule a visa interview appointment at the nearest US Embassy or Consulate.
  4. Attend the visa interview with all required documents.
  5. If approved, the passport with visa sticker is returned by post or for collection.

Skypass Visa Services assists with documentation preparation, DS-160 form guidance, cover letter drafting, travel itinerary preparation, and interview preparation.

Processing Time

Skypass Visa Services completes documentation preparation within a maximum of 5 working days. The US Embassy typically takes 15 to 25 working days after the interview to process the application and return the passport. Processing times may vary based on embassy workload and individual circumstances.

Common Reasons for Refusal

US B2 visas are frequently refused under Section 214(b) when the consular officer is not satisfied that the applicant has strong ties to India. Common reasons include:

  • Insufficient proof of financial stability or employment
  • Weak evidence of family, property, or other ties to India
  • Vague or inconsistent travel purpose
  • Previous visa refusals or immigration violations
  • Incomplete or inconsistent documentation

Skypass thoroughly reviews each applicant's profile before submission to identify and address potential concerns.

Visa Fee

The US Embassy charges a non-refundable MRV application fee (currently USD 185 for B1/B2 visa as of 2024, subject to change). This is a government fee paid directly to the US Embassy and is separate from Skypass service charges. Please verify the current fee on the US Embassy in India website before applying.
