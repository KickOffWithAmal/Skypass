What is the UAE Tourist Visa for Indians?

Indian passport holders require a UAE Tourist Visa (Visit Visa) to travel to Dubai, Abu Dhabi, or other Emirates for tourism or visiting friends and family. The UAE Tourist Visa is an e-visa applied for online through the ICA (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security) portal, via airlines such as Air Arabia, flydubai, or IndiGo, or through authorised travel agents. It is available as a 30-day or 60-day single-entry or multiple-entry visa.

Who Can Apply for a UAE Tourist Visa?

Any Indian national holding a valid passport may apply for a UAE Tourist Visa. The UAE authorities evaluate applications individually. Key factors for a strong application:

  • Valid Indian passport with at least 6 months validity from the date of travel
  • Sufficient funds to cover the full stay in the UAE
  • Clear purpose of visit (tourism, visiting family, transit)
  • No prior UAE visa overstay or immigration violations
  • Confirmed return tickets and accommodation

Note: Meeting these criteria does not guarantee visa approval. All visa decisions are made by UAE authorities.

Documents Required for UAE Tourist Visa from India

The following documents are typically required. Requirements may vary depending on the application channel (ICA portal, airline, or agent). Always verify before applying.

  • Valid Indian passport (minimum 6 months validity from travel date)
  • Recent passport-sized photograph (white background)
  • Confirmed return flight tickets
  • Hotel booking or accommodation proof (or host's UAE residence visa details if visiting family)
  • Bank statements for the last 3 months showing sufficient funds
  • Employment letter or proof of business registration
  • Travel insurance (recommended; may be required by some channels)

UAE Tourist Visa applications are typically processed within 3 to 5 working days for standard applications. Express processing (2 to 3 working days) may be available for an additional fee through certain channels. Skypass completes document preparation in up to 3 working days.

These are estimates. Processing times may vary. Apply at least 2 weeks before your travel date.

Common Reasons for UAE Visa Refusal

UAE Tourist Visa refusals can occur due to:

  • Prior UAE visa overstay or immigration violation
  • Passport with Israeli entry stamps (UAE has normalised relations with Israel since 2020, but some older cases may be flagged)
  • Incomplete or incorrect documents
  • Insufficient bank balance
  • Prior refusal by UAE or other Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries
  • No confirmed return tickets or accommodation

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about UAE visa services.

Do Indian passport holders need a visa to visit the UAE?
Yes, Indian passport holders require a UAE Tourist Visa (Visit Visa) to enter the UAE. The visa is an e-visa applied for online through the ICA portal, airlines, or authorised agents. There is no visa on arrival for Indian nationals at this time — the visa must be obtained before travel.
What documents are required for a UAE tourist visa from Kerala?
Key documents include: valid passport (6+ months validity from travel date), recent passport photograph, confirmed return flight tickets, hotel bookings or accommodation proof, bank statements for 3 months, and an employment letter or business proof. Travel insurance is recommended.
How long does UAE visa processing take from India?
UAE Tourist Visa applications are typically processed within 3 to 5 working days for standard processing. Express processing (2 to 3 working days) may be available at additional cost. Apply at least 2 weeks before your travel date.
What is the UAE tourist visa fee for Indian applicants?
UAE Tourist Visa fees vary by visa type and application channel. As a general guide: a 30-day single-entry visa costs approximately AED 300–500 (₹6,500–₹11,000), and a 60-day single-entry visa costs approximately AED 500–700 (₹11,000–₹16,000). Fees are subject to change — contact Skypass for current rates.
Can I extend my UAE tourist visa once inside the UAE?
Yes, UAE Tourist Visas can typically be extended for an additional 30 days through the ICA portal or via an authorised agent while you are inside the UAE. Extensions are subject to approval and applicable fees. Overstaying without an approved extension results in daily fines.
Is UAE visa on arrival available for Indian nationals?
No, there is currently no visa on arrival facility for Indian passport holders at UAE airports. Indian nationals must obtain a UAE Tourist Visa before travel, either through the ICA online portal, via airlines operating UAE routes, or through authorised visa agents like Skypass.
Why do UAE visa applications get refused?
Common refusal reasons include: prior UAE visa overstay, incomplete or incorrect documents, insufficient bank balance, prior refusal by UAE or other GCC countries, and no confirmed return tickets or accommodation.
