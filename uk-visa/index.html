What is the UK Standard Visitor Visa?

The UK Standard Visitor Visa allows Indian passport holders to visit the United Kingdom for tourism, visiting family or friends, attending conferences, or short business trips for up to 6 months per visit. Applications are processed by UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI).

Since 2024, the UK has transitioned to a digital entry clearance (e-visa) system. Most Indian nationals will receive a digital visa rather than a physical sticker in their passport. At the border, travellers present their passport and their eVisa permission is verified electronically.

Visa decisions are made solely by UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI). Approval cannot be guaranteed.

Who Can Apply?

Indian passport holders wishing to visit the UK for tourism, visiting family, attending events, short business trips, or transit may apply. Applicants must demonstrate sufficient funds, strong ties to India, and genuine intention to leave the UK at the end of their permitted stay.

Documents Required

Key documents for a UK Standard Visitor Visa from India typically include:

  • Valid original passport (minimum 6 months validity) and all previous passports
  • Personal bank statements for the last 6 months (bank-sealed original)
  • Income Tax Return (ITR-V) for the last 3 years
  • Employment certificate with salary, NOC, and approved leave letter (or business registration for self-employed)
  • Proof of funds, fixed deposits, or property documents
  • Detailed day-wise UK travel itinerary and confirmed hotel bookings
  • Strong evidence of ties to India (family, employment, property)
  • Sponsor or invitation letter with sponsor's bank statements (if visiting family or friends)

Requirements may change. Always verify with UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI).

Application Process

  1. Apply online via the UK government's visa application portal.
  2. Pay the UKVI visa application fee (currently £115 for a standard 6-month visitor visa, subject to change).
  3. Book a biometrics appointment at a VFS Global centre in India.
  4. Attend the VFS appointment and submit your documents.
  5. Wait for UKVI to process the application and receive the digital entry clearance (e-visa).

Processing Time

Skypass Visa Services completes documentation preparation within a maximum of 5 working days. UKVI standard processing is typically 3 to 6 weeks from biometric submission, though times may vary based on UKVI workload and individual application complexity.

Common Reasons for Refusal

UK visitor visa applications are frequently refused when UKVI is not satisfied of the applicant's genuine visitor intention. Common reasons include:

  • Insufficient evidence of financial capacity to fund the trip
  • Weak ties to India — insufficient evidence of employment, family, or property
  • Inconsistent or vague travel purpose
  • Incomplete document submission
  • Previous UK visa refusals or immigration overstays
