What is the New Zealand Visitor Visa?

Indian passport holders require a New Zealand Visitor Visa to enter New Zealand for tourism, visiting friends or relatives, or attending events. The application is submitted online through Immigration New Zealand (INZ) — there is no paper application or embassy queue. The Visitor Visa is an electronic visa (eVisa) linked to your passport and is typically granted for stays of up to 9 months, though the actual stay duration is determined at the border. The visa itself can be valid for up to 2 years for multiple trips.

Who Can Apply for a New Zealand Visitor Visa?

Any Indian national holding a valid passport may apply for a New Zealand Visitor Visa online. Immigration New Zealand evaluates each application individually. Factors that strengthen an application include:

  • Stable employment or business activity with approved leave
  • Sufficient personal savings to cover all expenses during the visit
  • Strong family ties in India (spouse, children, property, employment)
  • Prior international travel history (not mandatory but helpful)
  • A clear travel itinerary and confirmed accommodation
  • Valid travel insurance covering the full period of stay

Note: Meeting these criteria does not guarantee visa approval. All visa decisions are made by Immigration New Zealand.

Documents Required for New Zealand Visitor Visa from India

The New Zealand Visitor Visa is applied for online. The following supporting documents are typically uploaded during the application. Always check the Immigration New Zealand website for the latest document requirements.

  • Valid Indian passport (minimum 3 months validity beyond intended stay)
  • Recent passport-sized photograph meeting INZ specifications
  • Confirmed return flight tickets
  • Accommodation bookings or host's contact details (if staying with family/friends)
  • Bank statements for the last 3 to 6 months showing sufficient funds
  • Employment letter confirming salary, role, and approved leave (salaried applicants)
  • Business registration and income documents (self-employed applicants)
  • Travel insurance valid for New Zealand for the full visit period
  • Evidence of ties to India (property, family, employment contract)

New Zealand Visitor Visa Application Process from Kerala

The New Zealand Visitor Visa application is entirely online — there is no visa centre or embassy visit required (unless biometrics are requested). The general steps are:

  1. Consult Skypass — profile review and document gap analysis
  2. Document preparation — Skypass assists with checklisting and reviewing your file (up to 5 working days)
  3. Create INZ account — register on the Immigration New Zealand online portal
  4. Complete online application — fill in the Visitor Visa form and upload all documents
  5. Pay visa fee — NZD 211 (approximately ₹10,500–₹11,500) paid online by card
  6. INZ processing — typically 20 to 25 working days; INZ may request additional documents
  7. Receive eVisa — visa decision communicated by email; the eVisa is linked to your passport

New Zealand Visitor Visa Processing Time

Immigration New Zealand typically processes Visitor Visa applications from India within 20 to 25 working days, though this can vary. Skypass completes document preparation in up to 5 working days. Apply at least 6–8 weeks before your intended travel date to allow for any requests for additional information.

Processing times are estimates. INZ may take longer during peak seasons. Check current processing times on the INZ website before applying.

Common Reasons for New Zealand Visitor Visa Refusal

New Zealand Visitor Visa refusals often occur due to one or more of the following:

  • Insufficient funds relative to the planned length of stay
  • Weak ties to India — no stable employment, property, or dependants
  • Vague travel itinerary or unconfirmed accommodation
  • Missing or inadequate travel insurance
  • Inconsistencies between the application and supporting documents
  • Prior immigration breaches in New Zealand or other countries

What is the New Zealand visitor visa fee for Indian applicants?
The New Zealand Visitor Visa application fee is NZD 211 (approximately ₹10,500–₹11,500 at current rates). This is paid online directly to Immigration New Zealand during the application. There are no VFS or embassy charges for the standard online application.

Most Indian applicants for a New Zealand Visitor Visa do not need to submit biometrics for the standard online application. However, INZ may request biometrics in specific cases. If required, you would be directed to an authorised collection point. Skypass will advise if this applies to your profile.
How long can I stay in New Zealand on a visitor visa?
A New Zealand Visitor Visa typically allows a stay of up to 9 months per entry, though the Border Force officer at arrival makes the final determination on how long you may stay. The visa itself can be granted with validity of up to 2 years for multiple entries, depending on your profile.
