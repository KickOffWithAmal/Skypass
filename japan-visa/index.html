What is the Japan Temporary Visitor Visa?

Indian passport holders need a Temporary Visitor (Tanki-taizai) visa to visit Japan for tourism, sightseeing, visiting friends or relatives, or attending cultural events. It is typically issued as a single-entry sticker visa valid for stays of 15 or 30 days. Japan does not currently offer a standard tourist e-visa for Indian nationals — applications are submitted through the Embassy of Japan or VFS Global in India.

Who Can Apply for a Japan Tourist Visa?

Any Indian national holding a valid passport may apply for a Japan Temporary Visitor visa. The Embassy evaluates each application individually. Factors that strengthen an application include:

  • Stable employment or business with approved leave
  • Sufficient personal savings to cover all trip expenses
  • Strong family ties in India (spouse, children, property)
  • Prior international travel history (not mandatory)
  • A clear, day-by-day itinerary with confirmed bookings

Note: Meeting these criteria does not guarantee visa approval. The Embassy of Japan makes all visa decisions.

Documents Required for Japan Visa from India

The following documents are typically required. Requirements may vary — always verify with the Embassy of Japan or the official VFS Japan portal before applying.

  • Valid Indian passport (minimum 6 months validity beyond travel dates)
  • Completed Japan visa application form (available at Embassy or VFS)
  • Recent passport-sized photographs (white background, specific size)
  • Confirmed return flight tickets
  • Hotel bookings or accommodation proof for all nights
  • Bank statements for the last 3 to 6 months
  • Employment certificate or NOC from employer with approved leave letter
  • Detailed day-by-day Japan itinerary
  • Income tax returns (ITR) if applicable
  • Travel insurance (recommended)

Japan Visa Application Process from Kerala

Applications from Kerala are submitted through VFS Global Japan centres or the Embassy of Japan. The general steps are:

  1. Consult Skypass — profile review and document gap analysis
  2. Document preparation — Skypass assists with checklisting and reviewing your file (up to 5 working days)
  3. Book VFS appointment — walk-in or appointment-based depending on your location
  4. Biometric submission — Japan visa applications require fingerprints and a photograph at the VFS centre
  5. Embassy processing — typically 5 to 7 working days after submission
  6. Passport collection — collect from VFS or opt for courier return

Japan Visa Processing Time

The Embassy of Japan typically processes Temporary Visitor visa applications within 5 to 7 working days after the application is submitted to VFS Global. Skypass completes document preparation in up to 5 working days before submission. Total time from starting your file is approximately 10 to 15 working days, subject to Embassy workload and individual case complexity.

Processing times are estimates only. Embassy decisions may take longer. Apply well in advance of your intended travel date.

Common Reasons for Japan Visa Refusal

Japan visa refusals often occur due to one or more of the following:

  • Insufficient or inconsistent bank balance relative to trip cost
  • Vague or incomplete travel itinerary
  • Missing employer NOC or leave approval letter
  • No clear reason to return to India (weak ties)
  • Prior visa refusals not disclosed in the application
  • Photographs not meeting the Embassy's exact specifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Japan visa services.

What type of visa do I need to visit Japan from India?
Indian passport holders need a Japan Temporary Visitor (Tanki-taizai) visa for tourism, sightseeing, visiting family, or attending events. It is typically issued as a single-entry sticker visa for stays of 15 or 30 days. Japan does not currently offer a standard tourist e-visa for Indian nationals.
What documents are required for a Japan tourist visa from Kerala?
Key documents include: valid passport (6+ months validity), completed visa application form, passport photographs, confirmed return flight tickets, hotel bookings, bank statements for 3–6 months, employer NOC with leave approval, and a detailed day-by-day itinerary. Requirements can vary — always verify with the Embassy of Japan.
How long does Japan visa processing take from India?
After submission to VFS Global, the Embassy of Japan typically takes 5 to 7 working days to process a Temporary Visitor visa application. Skypass completes document preparation in up to 5 working days. Apply at least 3–4 weeks before your travel date to allow for any delays.
Do I need biometrics for a Japan visa?
Yes. Japan visa applications submitted through VFS Global require biometric data — fingerprints and a photograph — to be collected at the VFS centre in person. You must attend the VFS appointment yourself; this step cannot be completed remotely.
What is the Japan visa fee for Indian applicants?
The Japan visa fee for a single-entry Temporary Visitor visa is approximately ₹550–₹1,100 depending on the number of entries, plus VFS Global service charges. These fees are set by the Embassy and VFS and are subject to change. Contact Skypass for the current fee schedule.
Can I visit Japan without a visa if I have a US or Schengen visa?
No. Indian passport holders require a Japan visa regardless of holding other countries' visas. There is no visa-on-arrival facility for Indian nationals travelling to Japan. Apply in advance through the Embassy of Japan or VFS Global.
Why do Japan visa applications get refused?
Common refusal reasons include: insufficient bank balance for the trip, vague itinerary, missing employer NOC, weak ties to India, undisclosed prior refusals, and photographs not meeting Embassy specifications. A thorough document review before submission significantly reduces the risk of refusal.
