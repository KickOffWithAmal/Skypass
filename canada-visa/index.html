What is the Canada Visitor Visa (TRV)?

The Canada Temporary Resident Visa (TRV), commonly called a visitor visa, is required for Indian passport holders to enter Canada for tourism, visiting family or friends, or short business trips. Applications are processed by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).

The TRV can be issued as a single-entry or multiple-entry visa valid for up to 10 years or 1 month before your passport expires, whichever comes first. Each stay is typically limited to 6 months unless specified otherwise by IRCC.

Visa decisions are made solely by IRCC. Approval cannot be guaranteed.

Who Can Apply?

Indian passport holders who want to visit Canada for tourism, visiting family, attending events, or short business trips may apply for a TRV. Applicants must demonstrate sufficient funds, strong ties to India, and a clear intention to return home after the visit.

Documents Required

Key documents for a Canada TRV from India typically include:

  • Valid passport with at least 6 months validity and at least 2 blank pages
  • Completed IMM 5257 application form and IMM 5645 family information form
  • Recent passport-sized photographs meeting IRCC specifications
  • Bank statements for the last 6 months showing sufficient funds
  • Income tax returns (ITR) for the last 3 years
  • Employment letter with salary details, leave approval, and NOC
  • Confirmed flight itinerary and hotel bookings or invitation letter
  • Evidence of strong ties to India (property, family, employment)

Requirements may change. Always verify with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).

Application Process

  1. Create an IRCC online account and submit the application online.
  2. Pay the IRCC visa application fee (CAD 100 per person as of 2024, subject to change).
  3. Submit biometrics at a designated Visa Application Centre (VAC) in India if required.
  4. Upload or submit all required supporting documents.
  5. Wait for IRCC to process the application and receive the decision electronically.

Processing Time

Skypass Visa Services completes documentation preparation within a maximum of 5 working days. IRCC processing times vary but are typically 15 to 25 working days from submission. Processing times are subject to change based on IRCC workload.

Common Reasons for Refusal

Canada TRV applications are frequently refused when IRCC is not satisfied that the applicant will leave Canada at the end of their stay. Common reasons include:

  • Insufficient proof of financial capacity
  • Weak ties to India (employment, family, or property)
  • Vague or incomplete travel purpose and itinerary
  • Inconsistent or missing documents
  • Previous visa refusals or immigration violations

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Canada visa services.

What is a Canada Temporary Resident Visa (TRV)?
A Canada Temporary Resident Visa (TRV), also called a visitor visa, is required by Indian passport holders to enter Canada for tourism, visiting family or friends, or attending events. The application is submitted online through the IRCC (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada) portal using form IMM 5257. It is typically issued as a multiple-entry visa valid for up to 10 years or until your passport expires.
What documents are required for a Canada visitor visa from Kerala?
Key documents include: valid passport, completed IMM 5257 application form, recent photographs meeting IRCC specs, bank statements for 3–6 months, employment certificate or NOC with approved leave, Income Tax Returns for 2–3 years, confirmed return flight tickets, accommodation proof, and a detailed travel itinerary. Retired or self-employed applicants need additional documentation. Skypass will advise on your specific profile.
Do I need biometrics for a Canada visitor visa?
Yes. First-time applicants and those whose biometrics have expired must provide biometric data (fingerprints and photograph) at a designated Visa Application Centre (VAC). In Kerala, biometrics are collected at the VFS Global Canada VAC in Kochi or Thiruvananthapuram. The biometric fee is CAD 85 (approximately ₹5,300) per person.
How long does Canada visitor visa processing take from India?
IRCC's current processing time for visitor visa applications from India is typically 4 to 8 weeks, though it varies. Processing begins after biometrics are submitted. Skypass completes document preparation in up to 5 working days. Apply at least 8–12 weeks before your intended travel date to allow for delays.
What is the Canada visitor visa fee?
The IRCC application fee for a Canada Temporary Resident Visa is CAD 100 (approximately ₹6,200) per person. Biometrics cost an additional CAD 85 per person. These are government fees paid directly to IRCC online. Skypass charges a separate consultancy fee for document review and preparation assistance.
What financial proof do I need for a Canada visitor visa?
There is no officially mandated minimum bank balance for a Canada visitor visa. IRCC requires you to demonstrate sufficient funds to cover your stay and return travel. Typical financial documents include bank statements for 3–6 months, salary slips, Income Tax Returns, and property or investment documents. The adequacy of funds is assessed relative to your planned trip cost and duration.
Can my parents visit Canada on a visitor visa from India?
Yes. Retired parents can apply for a Canada visitor visa. For retired applicants, the application should demonstrate: pension income, savings, owned property in India, and a clear reason to return. If they have a child in Canada sponsoring their visit, a strong invitation letter from the host (with their status documents and finances) strengthens the application. Skypass can help prepare files for retired applicants.
What are common reasons for Canada visitor visa refusal?
Common refusal reasons include: insufficient ties to India (no stable employment, property, or dependants), inadequate funds relative to the trip cost, vague purpose of visit, prior refusals not disclosed, and incomplete or inconsistent documents. IRCC officers look for evidence that you will return to India after your visit.
