What Documents Do You Need for a Canada Visitor Visa?

Every Canada Temporary Resident Visa (TRV) application requires a core set of documents, plus additional documents depending on your employment type. The application is submitted online through the IRCC portal. Documents are uploaded as scanned copies — physical originals are not sent unless specifically requested by IRCC.

This checklist is a general guide. IRCC may request additional documents. Always verify current requirements on the official IRCC website.

Core Documents (Required for All Applicants)

The following documents are required regardless of your employment type:

  • Valid Indian passport — at least 6 months validity beyond your planned departure from Canada; include all previous passports if any
  • Completed IMM 5257 form — Temporary Resident Visa application, completed and signed electronically via the IRCC portal
  • Photographs — recent digital photograph meeting IRCC specifications (white background, specific size)
  • Confirmed return flight tickets — showing your arrival in Canada and return to India
  • Accommodation proof — hotel bookings for all nights, or an invitation letter from your Canadian host with their address and status documents
  • Detailed travel itinerary — day-by-day plan of where you will be in Canada and what you will do
  • Bank statements — 3 to 6 months of recent statements from your primary savings and salary account
  • Income Tax Returns — ITR-V or Acknowledgement for the last 2 to 3 years
  • Purpose of visit letter / cover letter — a brief letter explaining who you are, why you are visiting Canada, your itinerary, and your plans to return to India

Additional Documents — Salaried Employees

If you are employed with a company or organisation:

  • Employment certificate — on company letterhead, stating your designation, date of joining, current salary, and employment status (permanent / contract)
  • Leave approval letter — confirming your approved leave for the specific travel dates
  • Salary slips — last 3 months of pay slips
  • Form 16 — TDS certificate for the last financial year (if available)

Additional Documents — Self-Employed / Business Owners

If you run your own business:

  • Business registration certificate — GST registration, Shops and Establishments Act certificate, or company incorporation documents
  • Business bank statements — 3 to 6 months of business current account statements
  • Income Tax Returns — last 2 to 3 years, showing business income
  • CA-certified balance sheet and profit & loss statement — for the last 2 years (if applicable)
  • Letter from CA or accountant — confirming your business activity, annual turnover, and status as the owner

Additional Documents — Retired Applicants

If you are retired:

  • Pension certificate or pension slips — showing regular pension income from government or private employer
  • Bank statements — 6 months showing pension credits and overall savings
  • Property documents — property ownership (land, house) in India demonstrates ties to home country
  • Retirement letter or service certificate — from previous employer confirming retirement date and service history
  • Invitation letter — from your host in Canada (child or relative), including their IRCC status documents (PR card, work permit, or citizenship certificate)

Additional Documents — Students

If you are a student:

  • College / university enrollment letter — confirming your current enrollment, course name, and expected graduation date
  • Leave or vacation certificate — confirming your travel is during a holiday or approved break
  • Financial sponsor documents — if parents or a guardian are funding the trip, include their bank statements, ITR, employment documents, and a sponsorship letter
  • Parent/guardian cover letter — confirming they are sponsoring your visit and that you will return to complete your studies

Do I Need to Show a Minimum Bank Balance for a Canada Visa?

IRCC does not publish a universal minimum bank balance figure for visitor visa applicants. What matters is that your bank balance is consistent with your income and sufficient to cover your planned trip expenses.

Avoid depositing a large lump sum immediately before applying — IRCC officers look for organic, regular account activity. Statements showing consistent salary credits over several months are more convincing than a sudden large balance.

As a rough reference, consider that Canada is an expensive country to visit. A 2-week trip could easily cost ₹1.5–2.5 lakh or more including flights, accommodation, and daily expenses. Your financial documents should reflect your capacity to fund this.

Biometrics — What to Expect

After submitting your online application, IRCC will send a Biometric Instruction Letter (BIL) asking you to submit biometrics (fingerprints and photograph) at a VFS Global Canada VAC. Do not book your biometric appointment before receiving the BIL.

  • VFS Global Canada VAC locations in Kerala: Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram
  • Biometric fee: CAD 85 per person (approximately ₹5,300)
  • Biometrics are valid for 10 years — if you have given biometrics before and they are still valid, you do not need to give them again
