What is the Australia Subclass 600 Visitor Visa?

The Australia Subclass 600 Visitor Visa (Tourist Stream) is an e-visa that allows Indian passport holders to visit Australia for tourism, holidays, visiting family or friends, or short business trips. The visa is applied for online via the Australian Department of Home Affairs' ImmiAccount portal.

The Subclass 600 is typically valid for up to 1 year with multiple entries. Each stay is generally limited to 3 months, though longer stays can be requested.

Visa decisions are made solely by the Australian Department of Home Affairs. Approval cannot be guaranteed.

Who Can Apply?

Indian passport holders who wish to visit Australia for tourism, visiting family or friends, or short business activities may apply. Applicants must demonstrate sufficient funds, genuine tourist intent, and strong ties to India showing intention to return home.

Documents Required

Key documents for an Australia Subclass 600 visa from India typically include:

  • Valid passport with at least 6 months validity beyond intended stay
  • Completed online application via ImmiAccount
  • Recent passport-sized photographs
  • Bank statements for the last 6 months showing sufficient funds
  • Income tax returns for the last 3 years
  • Employment letter with salary, NOC, and leave approval
  • Confirmed flight itinerary and hotel bookings
  • Evidence of ties to India (family, employment, property)

Requirements may change. Always verify with the Australian Department of Home Affairs.

Application Process

  1. Create an ImmiAccount on the Australian Department of Home Affairs website.
  2. Complete and submit the Subclass 600 online application.
  3. Pay the visa application charge (currently AUD 190 as of 2024, subject to change).
  4. Upload all supporting documents as required.
  5. Wait for the Department of Home Affairs to process and grant the e-visa.

Processing Time

Skypass Visa Services completes documentation preparation within a maximum of 5 working days. The Australian Department of Home Affairs typically processes Subclass 600 applications within 20 to 40 calendar days. Processing times vary based on application volume and completeness.

Common Reasons for Refusal

Australia Subclass 600 applications are frequently refused when the Department is not satisfied of genuine visitor intent. Common reasons include:

  • Insufficient evidence of financial capacity
  • Weak ties to India — inadequate proof of employment, family, or property
  • Vague or inconsistent travel purpose
  • Incomplete document submission
  • Previous visa refusals or immigration compliance issues
